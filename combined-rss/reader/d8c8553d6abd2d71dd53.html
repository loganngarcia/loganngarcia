California voters reject Proposition 40, which would have imposed a one-time 5% tax on residents with a net worth above $1B to mostly help fund health care (Associated Press)

The measure would have imposed a one-time 5% tax on the wealth of Californians whose net worth exceeds $1 billion. It would have been the first tax of its kind in the nation.
