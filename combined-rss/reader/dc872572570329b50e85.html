Spines, which uses AI agents to edit and proofread amateur authors' books, design their covers, and handle the layout, raised $29M in equity and $35M in debt (Assaf Gilead/Globes)

The Herzliya-based company’s platform allows authors to upload their manuscripts to the platform, where the company’s AI agents edit and proofread the text, design the cover, and handle the book’s layout.
