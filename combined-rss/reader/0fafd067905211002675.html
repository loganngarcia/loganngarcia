Scale Labs and CAIS' Remote Labor Index: Fable 5.2 completes 41.3% of freelance projects at professional quality, above Opus 5.5's 21.3% and GPT-6 Astra's 20.8% (Maximilian Schreiner/The Decoder)

Anthropic's Fable 5.2 completes 41.3 percent of real freelance jobs at professional quality on the Remote Labor Index, more than double the next-best systems Opus 5.5 and GPT 6 Astra. A year ago the top score was 2.5 percent. Areas like product design and animation remain a weak spot.
