Qwen-Image-Layered: Layered Decomposition for Inherent Editablity

Today, we are excited to introduce Qwen-Image-Layered, a model capable of decomposing an image into multiple RGBA layers. This layered representation unlocks inherent editability: each layer can be independently manipulated without affecting other content.
