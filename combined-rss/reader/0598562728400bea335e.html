Robbyant Open-Sources LingBot-Touch, One Tactile Model for Three Sensor Types

Ant Group's Robbyant has released LingBot-Touch under Apache 2.0. Across 26 real-robot tasks, its tactile tokens lifted four policies by 14 to 25 points, including on a hand whose sensor it never saw.
