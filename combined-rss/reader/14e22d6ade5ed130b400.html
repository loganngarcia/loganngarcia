NJIT's PHD-Hand Uses Linkages, Not Tendons, in a Humanoid Hand You Can Print for Under $650

NJIT researchers built PHD-Hand, a 570 g, 3D-printable humanoid hand with 15 linkage-driven joints and a parts cost under $650. The paper is accepted at IEEE Humanoids 2026, and the design will be open-sourced.
