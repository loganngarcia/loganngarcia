Business intelligence startup Veridion, which develops an AI-powered, real-time map of ~640M businesses worldwide, raised a $20M Series A led by Hoxton Ventures (Tamara Djurickovic/Tech.eu)

The Series A investment will support the development of Veridion’s AI-powered business intelligence platform, providing organisations with more up-to-date information on companies and commercial changes worldwide.
