Sources: NSA Net, the highly classified network that the NSA uses for its internal communications, is down; there is no evidence of a cyberattack (CNN)

The highly classified network that the National Security Agency uses for its internal communications is down, according to six people familiar with the matter. The NSA closed its campus to most employees on Tuesday night, reverting to essential personnel only as it works to restore the network, according to a voicemail inbox used by NSA employees and a source familiar with the matter.
