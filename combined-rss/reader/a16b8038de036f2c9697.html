Andy Rubin's Genki Robotics Shows Its Humanoid to Japan's Prime Minister

Genki Robotics, the Tokyo startup founded by Android creator Andy Rubin, showed its humanoid for the first time at Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi's physical AI meeting. The unit walked for the first time three days earlier, and Genki plans an open Android-style platform built on Japanese hardware.
