Bites, whose AI tools deliver personalized training to frontline workers via WhatsApp, SMS, Slack, and other channels, raised a $40M Series A led by PeakSpan (Meir Orbach/CTech)

The platform turns company procedures into short, branded videos delivered through WhatsApp, Teams and other familiar channels. The company plans to expand in the U.S. and develop technology linking training to business outcomes.
