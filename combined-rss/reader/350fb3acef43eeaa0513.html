Nvidia's board establishes a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee to oversee national security policies, export control compliance, and more (Nvidia Newsroom)

NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) today announced that the Board of Directors has established the National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee, a new standing committee responsible for board-level oversight of the company’s national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risk management.
