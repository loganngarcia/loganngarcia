Hadrian, an agentic AI offensive security service, raised $40M co-led by Forgepoint Capital International and SmartFin, taking its total funding to $65M (Tamara Djurickovic/Tech.eu)

Hadrian will use the funding to expand across EMEA and the US while growing its engineering and research teams and further developing its agentic AI platform for offensive security.
