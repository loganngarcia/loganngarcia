NYC-based Confido, a provider of AI-powered workflow automation tools for consumer packaged goods companies, raised a $55M Series B led by Insight Partners (AlleyWatch)

For decades, CPG software recorded the work but never did it, says Confido Cofounder and CEO Justin Hunter. Closing that gap between logging a problem and actually fixing it is what convinced 250+ CPG brands, from Dude Wipes to Unilever, to run their back office through Confido. The company just closed a $55M Series B led by Insight Partners, bringing total funding to $77M, on the back of 5x year-over-year growth.…
