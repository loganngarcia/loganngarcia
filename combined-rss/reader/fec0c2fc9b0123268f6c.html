Sources: India-based Slice, which has transitioned from a credit card-led fintech to a small finance bank, raised $100M at a ~$450M valuation, down from ~$1.4B (Moneycontrol)

The downround, which marks a nearly 70% correction from Slice’s earlier valuation, comes after funding talks with Accel, Elevation Capital and Peak XV Partners, as reported by Moneycontrol, failed to materialise.
