After SuperMimic, Open-Source Humanoid Motion Trackers Now Train in Minutes on One GPU

A UC Berkeley paper released without code set off a run of community reimplementations. InstantTrack, BlinkMimic and XMimic cut Unitree G1 tracker training on a 40-clip benchmark to a few minutes, in simulation.
