IntelliProve Raises €2.7M to Bring Camera-Based Vital Signs to Humanoid Robots

The Belgian startup reads heart rate, breathing and blood pressure from a 30-second face scan with an ordinary camera. After building it into virtual-care apps in 50+ countries, it now wants the same software running on humanoid and assistive robots.
