GlobalFoundries Becomes First Chipmaker to Test Boston Dynamics' Atlas

GlobalFoundries joins the Atlas early adopter program, the first customer from outside the car industry, with onsite testing from early 2027. It is also spreading Spot to fabs in New York, Dresden and Singapore, where two robots have logged 90,000 inspections since April.
