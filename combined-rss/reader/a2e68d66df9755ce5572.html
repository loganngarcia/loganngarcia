South Korea Commits KRW 2.3 Trillion to Build Full-Stack Humanoid Robotics Ecosystem by 2030

South Korea is allocating $1.66 billion directly to humanoid robotics and $2.0 billion for physical AI field verification, targeting an 80% domestic component localization rate and state procurement of over 1,000 bipedal units.
